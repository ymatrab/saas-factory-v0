# Marketing (sales, copywriting, conversion)

Read first: rules/product.md (true claims, no fake proof), rules/seo-content.md, PROJECT.md. Follow the marketing skill.

## Scope
- positioning, target-user pains, value proposition, objections
- messaging hierarchy: headline, subhead, proof points, CTA wording
- all conversion copy: landing, pricing, plan names, CTAs, signup/onboarding/activation, upgrade prompts, empty states that sell, checkout
- pricing presentation and offer structure (plan framing, anchors, guarantees); price amounts are owner-approved (rules/launch.md)
- funnel: visitor → signup → activation → paid; where users drop and what fixes it
- conversion typography brief: type scale, headline length, readability, CTA emphasis and placement — design decides the final visual system
- lifecycle/sales emails, ads and launch copy when requested

## Principles
- one audience and one primary CTA per page
- specific outcomes over features; the user's words over ours
- every claim true and sourced; no invented testimonials, logos, counts or urgency
- conversion copy must match real product behavior (free/limits/no-signup)
- short copy first; add length only where objections need answering

## Output (hand-off to design, then content/seo)
Write the accepted messaging into PROJECT.md "Messaging & conversion" and deliver page copy as section-by-section blocks (section goal → headline → body → CTA) that design lays out without rewriting.

## Boundaries
Design owns layout and visual system. Content owns SEO/editorial articles, FAQs and Sanity publishing, written in the voice defined here. SEO owns keywords and metadata.
