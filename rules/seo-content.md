# SEO & Content Rules

- Search pages must have distinct intent and useful content.
- Do not produce thin pages just for keyword coverage.
- Use factual, direct answers suitable for users and answer engines.
- Conversion copy should remain specific to the actual product.
- Avoid AI filler and unsupported superlatives.

## Learned rules
- **Target creation intent.** Prioritize queries where the user wants to make or do something with the product. Retrieval and definition queries can rank top-10 with ~0 clicks.
- **No per-variant pages without demand.** No page per template/style/variant unless measured demand exists and each page can be genuinely distinct.
- **Fix pages, don't delete them.** Rewrite underperforming pages. 301 only competitor pages and true duplicates. State impressions and position before proposing any removal.
- **Per-page sitemap dates.** A visible content change moves that page to a fresh `lastModified`. Never restamp untouched pages.
- **Cite and source.** Regulatory claims cite an authority and carry a not-advice note. Competitor figures come from the competitor's live page.
- **Stay legitimate.** Exclude keywords whose intent contradicts what the product legitimately is (e.g. fake documents, misuse). Do not build "what we are not" pages to capture them.
- **Review gate for regulated content.** Pages stating tax, legal or financial facts stay noindex and out of sitemap, llms.txt and nav until a review record exists (builder skill).
